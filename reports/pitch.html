PROBLEM FRAMING

Raw accuracy is the wrong target.

State-of-the-art hand gesture systems report 99%+ on benchmarks. But they're evaluated assuming the model must always predict.

BENCHMARK

99%

Raw classification accuracy on a clean test set.

→

DEPLOYED

FAIL

A confident "ALL CLEAR" when a user is signalling distress.

OUR POSITION: Selective accuracy — accuracy conditional on the model choosing to respond — is the right evaluation target for high-stakes accessibility tools.

LIMITATIONS & ETHICS

Three honest limitations.

  • DATASET BIAS HaGRID is primarily Eastern European subjects. Cross-demographic performance has not been audited. Pre-deployment requirement.
  • NO PARTICIPATORY DESIGN Vocabulary was curated by us, not co-designed with deaf, hard-of-hearing, or aphasic communities the system targets.
  • RESEARCH PROTOTYPE — NOT A MEDICAL DEVICE Selective prediction reduces, not eliminates, misrecognition risk. Production deployment requires human-in-the-loop verification.
